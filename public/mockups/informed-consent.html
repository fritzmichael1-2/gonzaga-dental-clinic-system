1. Treatment to be done.I authorize the attending dentist and staff to perform the dental procedures explained to me — examination, cleaning, fillings, crowns, bridges, extractions, root canal treatment, dentures, and the administration of local anesthesia. The nature, alternatives, and risks of these procedures have been explained to me and my questions have been answered. Initial:
2. Drugs & medications.Antibiotics, analgesics, and local anesthetics given in the course of treatment may cause redness, swelling, pain, itching, vomiting, or, rarely, anaphylactic shock. I have disclosed all allergies and all medication I am currently taking. Initial:
3. Changes in treatment plan.I authorize the dentist to make modifications found necessary during active care, such as performing a root canal on a tooth originally intended for a filling, and I accept the corresponding additional fee. Initial:
4. Radiograph.I consent to diagnostic X-rays. I understand that radiographs are not infallible and may not reveal every condition present, and that treatment may be adjusted once the tooth is examined directly. Initial:
5. Removal of teeth.Extraction may involve pain, swelling, bleeding, infection, dry socket, fractured jaw, or numbness. I confirm that the alternatives to extraction and the consequences of not removing the tooth have been discussed with me. Initial:
6. Crowns (caps) & bridges.Crowns and bridges may cause temporary sensitivity, and the final shade may differ slightly from natural teeth. I will care for the temporary restoration and return for permanent cementation within the time advised, failing which it may break or fail. Initial:
7. Endodontics (root canal).Root canal treatment may involve instrument breakage, perforation, or flare-up, and its success cannot be guaranteed. A tooth that cannot be saved may still require extraction. Initial:
8. Periodontal disease.Inflammation of the gums and supporting bone may persist or recur despite treatment and may lead to tooth loss. Periodontal treatment options, including the option of no treatment, have been explained to me. Initial:
9. Fillings.A restored tooth may be sensitive to heat, cold, or pressure, which usually settles with time. I will avoid chewing hard food on it for 24 hours and understand that a fractured filling may later require root canal treatment or a crown. Initial:
10. Dentures.New dentures require an adjustment period and may cause sore spots that need correction. Relines, remakes, or rescheduled delivery caused by missed appointments may involve additional fees. Initial:

I understand that dentistry is not an exact science and that no dentist can properly guarantee accurate results all the time.

I authorize the attending dentist and staff of Gonzaga Dental Clinic to perform the treatment described above and any procedure necessary in their professional judgement. I accept full responsibility for payment of all fees for services rendered, regardless of any insurance coverage. I confirm that I have read and understood this form, that my questions have been answered, and that I give my consent voluntarily, under my own free will, and may withdraw it at any time.
